Life Dashboard

A personal Mac app by Joey Summers that keeps his day, messages, home and projects in one place.

What it does

  • Shows today at a glance: tasks, calendar, habits, goals and a morning briefing.
  • Reads Joey's Gmail inbox so he can see and search his mail without leaving the app.
  • Runs a full-screen assistant called Joey that he can talk or type to.
  • Controls his home, printer, music and Mac from one screen.

Who it's for

Life Dashboard is built by Joey for his own use on his own Mac. It is not sold or offered to the public.

Google sign-in

When Joey connects his Google account, Life Dashboard asks for read-only access to Gmail, and, only if he turns it on, Google Assistant to control his Google Home devices. How that data is handled is set out in the privacy policy.
