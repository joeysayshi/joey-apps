Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Life Dashboard is a personal Mac app made by Joey Summers for his own use. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What Google data it uses

  • Gmail (read only): the app reads messages in the signed-in inbox so they can be shown, searched and summarised in the app. It cannot send, delete or change mail.
  • Google Assistant (optional): only if turned on in Settings, the app sends typed or spoken commands such as "turn on the lights" to Google Assistant to control Google Home devices.

Where it's kept

Sign-in tokens are kept on the Mac in a file only that Mac user can read. Mail is fetched from Google when needed and is not copied to any server run by Life Dashboard. There is no Life Dashboard server.

Sharing

Google data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. When the user asks the built-in assistant about their mail, the relevant text is sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) only to answer that request.

Limited use

Life Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect Google in the app's Settings, or remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
